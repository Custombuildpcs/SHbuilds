About Steven Hanna · SH Builds

The person behind every build.

My name is Steven Hanna, and I am the founder of Custom Build PCs. With five years of hands-on experience in custom computer building and hardware services, I have developed a strong understanding of computer components, system configurations, troubleshooting, and the process of creating computers tailored to individual needs.

What started as an interest in computer hardware developed into a business built around helping people get the most out of their technology.

Throughout my experience, I have built more than 50 custom computers, working with different hardware configurations, performance requirements, and budgets. Each build presents an opportunity to combine technical knowledge with attention to detail, from selecting compatible components to assembling and configuring the finished system.

Beyond building computers, I provide repair and upgrade services, helping customers improve existing systems, resolve hardware issues, and find practical solutions rather than unnecessarily replacing their computers.

I believe a good computer should not require an unreasonable price tag. My approach is centered on understanding what each customer needs, recommending suitable hardware, and delivering quality work.

Custom Build PCs represents my commitment to turning an interest in technology into practical solutions that people can rely on.

My approach to computer building

Every build starts with what the computer needs to do and what you want to spend. From there I select compatible components, assemble the system, manage the cabling, and configure it so it is ready to use.

My commitment to customers

I recommend hardware that fits your needs rather than the most expensive option, and I look for practical fixes before suggesting a replacement. Affordable pricing and quality workmanship apply to every project.
